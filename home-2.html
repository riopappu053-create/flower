Care Guide

Make your blooms last longer

Four simple habits that add days — sometimes weeks — to the life of any bouquet.

  1. 01

    Trim the Stems

    Cut 2 cm off each stem at a 45° angle before placing in water — it helps them drink faster.

  2. 02

    Change the Water

    Refresh the water every two days and add the flower food sachet that comes with your bouquet.

  3. 03

    Keep Them Cool

    Avoid direct sunlight, radiators, and fruit bowls — all three shorten a bouquet's life.

  4. 04

    Remove Wilting Petals

    Pull off any fading petals to keep the rest of the arrangement looking crisp and fresh.
